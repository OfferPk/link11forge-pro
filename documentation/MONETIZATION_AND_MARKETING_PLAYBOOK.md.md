# Link11Forge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

Link 11 (TADIL-A) is installed across hundreds of allied naval warships, maritime patrol aircraft, and coastal defense batteries worldwide. As modern naval operations bridge legacy Link 11 networks into Link 16 and Link 22 multi-TDL architectures (via gateways such as the NATO Link 11/16/22 Gateway), defense contractors face steep hardware terminal licensing fees (\$30,000 to \$75,000 per workstation). Link11Forge Pro provides an accessible, browser-native development and compliance verification environment for naval defense contractors, avionics software engineers, and tactical data link test facilities.
